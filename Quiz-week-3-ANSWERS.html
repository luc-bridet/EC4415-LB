RegionalFoodTaxes_A

A country produces food in two regions, West and East. The two regional supply curves and the national demand curve are displayed in the graph. There is one national food market, so consumers pay a common market price. In the absence of taxes, producers in West and East also receive that common price.

The policy notation is:

  • C[t]: an undifferentiated consumption tax of t per unit. Consumers pay the market price, while producers in both regions receive the market price minus the tax.
  • WL[t]: a production tax of t per unit on food produced in West. Western producers receive the market price minus the tax, while Eastern producers receive the market price.

Give numerical answers rounded to the nearest integer. There is a tolerance due to graphical methods being approximate.

Do not type units, currency symbols, thousands separators, or abbreviations. For example, type 10000 rather than 10,000, 10K, or 10000 euros.

You must answer every question. If you think a question is ambiguous, answer using your best interpretation and explain the issue in the optional comments box at the end.

Section E [8] Interpretation and policy choice

Question 27 [2.0]  

Interpret the deadweight loss of consumption tax C[163]. Use either a surplus-accounting approach or a unit-by-unit approach, but not both. Write no more than 3 sentences.

Answer
[2.0]  

Unit-by-unit:
Units q=3510 through q=4142 contribute ∫ v(q) - c(q), q= 3510 .. 4142 , where v(.) is the marginal WTP on the demand curve, and c(q) is the marginal cost, on the untaxed aggregate supply curve. 51531.

A surplus-accounting answer is based on adding up all the relevant components of surplus and creating total welfare. It shows that the government raises 572149 while causing a loss in CS of 916924 - 515257 = 401667, causing a loss in PS of 823966 - 601953 = 222013. The DWL is the difference between what producers lose and what the government gains: 401667 +222013 - 572149 = 51531. In tax settings, the DWL is also known as the excess burden of taxation

The PS loss can be further split as a loss in PS to W producers 538720 - 414378 = 124342, and a loss in PS to E producers 285247 - 187575 = 97672, but that is not relevant to total DWL.

A graphical representation borrows from both approaches: it is based on surplus calculations, but it also represents the DWl area as being generated by units q=3510 through q=4142.
Question 28 [2.0]  

Discuss the normative analysis of the policy choice between consumption taxes C[163] and C[648]. Write no more than 2 sentences.

Answer
[2.0]  

C[163] raises government surplus of approximately 572149 and creates deadweight loss of approximately 51531. C[648] raises government surplus of approximately 421823 and creates deadweight loss of approximately 1265038.
So C[648] is on the wrong side of the Laffer curve and has disproportionately high excess burden.

Question 30 [2.0]  

Interpret the deadweight loss of WL[282] using a unit-by-unit approach. Write no more than 4 sentences. Hint: your analysis of C[163] should help.

Answer
[2.0]  

The Western tax prevents some food units for which consumers' willingness to pay exceeds the real cost of production. Since the drop in consumption is the same as C[163], the consumption DWL is 51531.

The Western tax also displaces some production from West to East, including instances when the displaced Western units have lower real production cost than their Eastern replacements. We know that the DWL adds up to 113195 so the production DWL must equal 113195.

Quantifying exactly the production DWL from first principles and representing them as an area is tricky, and more than can be expected in an exam, but it can be done and is perhaps instructive.

The displaced portion of the Supply W curve has marginal cost between 339 and 458. The added portion of the Supply E curve has marginal cost between 458 and 621. Both segments correspond to 576 units. Hence a sensible measure of the production DWL is the area between these supply curves segments, shifting them horizontally to make them line up.
