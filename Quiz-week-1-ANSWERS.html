FoodLandRent_A

This is a variant of the "Land rent and international trade" exercise with different parameter values (different curves). The world supply is perfectly elastic at a price of 110 per unit.
The different policy labels are the same than in the online solution: for example, Autarky0 refers to the competitive equilibrium under autarky using the initial demand curve (Demand0), and FreeTrade1 refers to the competitive equilibrium under free trade using the post-shock demand curve (Demand1).

Quizzes are machine-graded. You need to provide an answer, and you need to use your best judgement for how to interpret a question. If you think a question is ambiguous, you can explain in the free-text question at the end of the quiz, but you need to answer anyway.

Since graphical coordinates are rounded to the nearest integer and some questions require graphical construction, all numerical answers have a tolerance and are counted as correct as long as they are close enough to the correct value. Slope readings and questions requiring you to construct new curves involve more approximation than coordinate readings, and have larger tolerances.

Check that your answers have the correct order of magnitude: if you forget a zero or add one too many, you will be off by a factor of 10, so the tolerance will not apply (nor should it!).

To avoid encoding errors, do not type units (kg, £, $, € ...) or multiples, type integers without any thousands separator: to answer "How many square metres in a hectare?", type 10000 and not 10 000 or 10,000 or 10K or 10000 m2 or 10000 m2.
